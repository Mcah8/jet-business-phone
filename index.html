Features

Enterprise phone features. Without the enterprise price tag.

Visual Callflow Builder

Drag-and-drop to build any call routing scenario — IVR menus, time-based routing, geographic routing and more. Make changes yourself in minutes, no IT required.

IVR Menus

Interactive voice response menus let callers self-select — "Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support." Multi-level menus, custom greetings, and business hours rules all included.

Geographic Routing

Route inbound calls to the right office automatically, based on the caller's geographic location. Ideal for businesses with multiple locations across Australia.

Postcode IVR

Ask callers to enter their postcode and route them to the nearest branch automatically — a seamless experience that eliminates manual transfers and reduces handle time.

Call Queueing

Keep callers in a queue with hold music and position announcements when your team is busy. Set overflow rules, maximum wait times, and callback options.

Real-Time Reporting

See all call activity in real-time — inbound volumes, answer rates, wait times, agent performance, and missed calls. Export for analysis or compliance at any time.

Local, 1300 & 1800 Numbers

Choose from local geographic numbers (02, 03, 07, 08), national 1300 numbers, or free-to-caller 1800 numbers. Port your existing numbers at no charge.

Call Recording

Record calls automatically for compliance, training, and quality assurance. Recordings are stored securely in the cloud and accessible from your portal at any time.
