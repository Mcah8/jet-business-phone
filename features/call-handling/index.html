Features Call Handling

Full control over how every call is managed.

Configure hold music, call queues, overflow rules, and caller experience — all from one place.

Book a Demo Talk to Sales

Call Handling covers everything that happens once a call hits your phone system — before it's answered and while it's being managed. Hold music, queue positions, overflow routing, call waiting, warm transfers — it all sits under call handling. Done right, it means callers have a professional experience from start to finish, and your team has the tools to manage calls efficiently.

Key Benefits

Call queuing

Callers wait in a queue rather than hitting a busy signal. Manage queue depth, hold messages, and estimated wait times.

Hold music and messages

Replace silence with branded hold music or informational messages while callers wait.

Warm and blind transfers

Transfer calls with a quick introduction (warm) or directly to a team member (blind) — whatever suits the situation.

Overflow routing

When a queue hits capacity or wait times exceed a threshold, calls automatically redirect to overflow numbers or voicemail.

Call waiting and do not disturb

Give team members control over their availability without dropping callers.

Consistent caller experience

Every person who calls your business gets the same professional, structured experience — regardless of which team member picks up.
